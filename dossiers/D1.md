## Flags from the source review (Session 6)
- U12 Article 23 ("adhere to the following principles") does not show a notice duty for automated or AI-assisted decisions: Article 23(4)(a) is about informing people which entity will collect their data. It is listed above for completeness and is NOT treated as supporting the notice requirement.
- U15 Specification DE6 is a redress mechanism, not notice, and the Smart Data Framework is non-binding in the knowledge base metadata; it cannot establish a duty.
- Since the Session 6 retrieval path the recorded searches also return U01 Articles 13 and 18 (Arabic print edition). On the author's reading, Article 13 lists among the information a data subject may request the decisions taken on the basis of automated processing, including profiling, and Article 18 gives a right to object to such decisions. The skeleton's earlier wording "none found in the reviewed UAE instruments" was therefore too strong: the better reading is that the federal personal data law has such provisions but, by its Article 2(2), appears not to apply to government entities. This is a reading of Arabic text, not a verified quotation.
- The scope finding rests on the Arabic print edition of U01 only. It is the author's reading of the Arabic text, not a verified quotation, and its English wording has not been obtained.
- Nothing in this skeleton is a supported policy finding. The conclusion "unresolved evidence" stands; the source-checked dossier for S-01 is docs/DOSSIER_S01.md (step 2).

## Requirement at issue
A person who is the subject of an AI-assisted pre-decision about a public benefit is told that an automated pre-check was used and can obtain an explanation of its role in the outcome.

## Existing provisions in the reviewed instruments
- U12 Article 23 [U12-9032fac242]: "adhere to the following principles"
- U15 Part: Specification DE6 [U15-881075db8a]: "provide complaints and redress mechanisms for data subjects"
- U01 Article 13 [U01-95596d05e4, U01-3c25b11ac7]
- U01 Article 18 [U01-a42384216c]

## Scope finding
U01 Article 18 gives a right to object to decisions of automated processing with legal or significant effect, but Article 2(2)(a) and (b) exclude government data and government entities that control or process personal data. On the Arabic print edition this reading is confirmed; the English wording is not yet obtained.
