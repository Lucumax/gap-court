## Requirement at issue
Records that an emirate-level entity collected for one purpose are not used by a federal entity for a different purpose without a stated legal basis and the person's knowledge.

## Existing provisions in the reviewed instruments
- U06 Article 15 [U06-5255b60b5e]: "Dubai Data may be used only for its intended purposes"
- U12 Article 17 [U12-2cc6c915a2]: "disclosing Personal Data (excluding Sensitive Personal Data) for a purpose other than that for which it is collected"
- U12 Article 23 [U12-9032fac242]: "seek the consent of individuals and Private Entities to use, store, process, and exchange"
- U06 Article 3 [U06-86b7213ed7]: "Federal Government Entities which have any Data relating to the Emirate"

- U13 Article 8 [U13-84807e072e]: "without prejudice to the rules and scope of legal protection of Personal Data"

## Preliminary conclusion (agent-drafted)
existing coverage found, with an unresolved question about which regime governs the federal recipient. Purpose limitation and consent to exchange are stated at the emirate level and the federal policy level. U06 Article 3 names federal entities that hold emirate data, while U08 applies to federal entities. No reviewed text says how the two combine. This is an ambiguity (type 3), not an absence.
