## Requirement at issue
A person can obtain a human review of, and challenge, an AI-assisted benefit decision, within stated time limits, through a body that is not the deciding office.

## Existing provisions in the reviewed instruments
- U12 Article 34 [U12-eb55db055b]: "within fifteen (15) Working Days from the date of being notified of the contested decision"
- U11 Article 6 [U11-a6aa02a6e5]: "consider and determine the grievances submitted to the DDSE"
- U06 Article 6 [U06-d3077f48be]: "investigate the complaints and violations related to Data Providers compliance"
- U15 Part: Specification DE6 [U15-881075db8a]: "provide complaints and redress mechanisms for data subjects"
- U16 Section 8 [U16-06ff8cf8b9]: "In case of inaccurate or incorrect data, the user can refer back to the entity"

## Scope finding
U01 Article 18(4) requires the controller to bring a human into review of automated decisions on request, but Article 2(2)(b) excludes government entities (Arabic print edition; English wording not yet obtained).
