PC05

Policy and legal adviser Allow (a proposal)

Under U12 Article 23, a Government Entity must seek the consent of individuals before using, storing, processing, or exchanging their Personal Data with other Government Entities in the Emirate, and must provide options to amend data or revoke that consent. The U01 scope clause excludes government entities that control or process personal data, so the federal PDPL does not govern this exchange.

“seek the consent of individuals and Private Entities to use, store, process, and exchange with other Government Entities in the Emirate their Personal Data”
