PC01

Policy and legal adviser Deny (a proposal)

U06 Article 15 states that Dubai Data may be used only for its intended purposes, which prohibits use for a different purpose. U12 Article 23 and U12 Article 17 reinforce this by requiring data to be used for specific, explicitly stated purposes and by treating disclosure of Personal Data for a purpose other than collection as a confidentiality harm.
