Preamble text of the synthetic law. It sets the stage.

### Article 1
In this Law, "Agency" means any ministry or department of the synthetic state.

### Article 2
1. This Law applies to the processing of personal data by private companies.
2. This Law does not apply to:
a. government agencies that process personal data; or
b. data held for security purposes.
<!--page:2-->
3. A private company must give a person notice before an automated decision about that person is made.

### Article 3
An agency that shares benefit records with another agency must record the purpose of the sharing. The recipient must not use the records for a different purpose without a written basis. The record of purpose must be kept for the period set by regulation.
